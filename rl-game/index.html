The Whip

A party whip has 15 moves to work the Capitol before the roll call. Three members are wavering and one is loyal. Secure all three wavering votes (3 of the 4 members) to win. Play it yourself, then set the points and watch a reinforcement learning agent figure out what those points reward.

The points

Reward scheme

These numbers feed reward() directly and apply from the next move. A trained agent keeps its habits and may never notice new points, so press Reset agent before you test a change.

Learning curve

Each point is 50 training games. Training games include the agent's random exploring, so they run below what Watch it play shows. The goal share is counted from the votes themselves, never from the points.
