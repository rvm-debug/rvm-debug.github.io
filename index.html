Project interests

I am drawn to many different potential projects for this class. The Canaries reading has me interested in this idea of “female-oriented” entry-level careers being the most affected by advancements in AI, and it raises relevant questions about gender politics in the workplace. For example: being referred to as a growth engineer (more men) vs. a marketer (more female), or bizops/strategy (more men) vs. operations/compliance (more female).

I am also drawn to:

  • Research on AI and its effects on climate/water supply, as I have heard conflicting hypotheses about this aspect of AI advancement.
  • Patching discrepancies between the AI available on the market vs. AI in labs. For example: how can we determine what tools can be available for the general public vs. confined to maximum security labs?

Finally, I am interested in discussing a topic mentioned by Dr. Xu: the idea that AGI has found ways to replicate the genetic and nurtured diversity of human brains. When asked what AI can’t do, I suggested that AI cannot currently represent the mass differences in brain chemistry, thought, and experience across the global human population. However, Dr. Xu said this is possible as of last year. Would love to learn more.
